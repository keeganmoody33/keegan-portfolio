**What I actually did — 2025–2026:**

- Provisioning inboxes, configuring cold email systems, and setting up sending infrastructure
- Built target lists across four verticals: bariatric surgery, medical weight loss, HRT, and lifestyle medicine

- Generated $200K+ in early-stage pipeline and 20+ net-new leads

**What the work was actually like:**

- Heavy on strategy and advising; not always a clean handoff to internal execution
- BCOFA leadership is overcommitted and runs the company evenings/weekends around surgical schedules
- Technical advisor Brian Briggman asked sharp, qualifying questions — had to sell the plan to both a non-technical founder and a technical filter
- The work was about enabling them to run outbound, not running full campaigns end-to-end myself

**What I learned:**

- Healthcare SaaS outbound requires compliance-forward messaging and patience around clinical credibility
- Founder-led sales with limited bandwidth means enablement has to be simple and durable
- B2B healthcare ICPs are often finite and enumerable — once the list is built, the motion becomes maintenance and personalization
- Working with surgeons as stakeholders means compressed windows and high-context communication

**Honest context:**

The 2024 engagement was consultative and strategic; the 2025–2026 re-engagement was a focused, roughly one-month infrastructure-and-advising sprint with ongoing weekly check-ins. I built the systems and targeting, but the long-term execution depended on BCOFA's internal capacity. Metrics ($200K pipeline, 20+ leads) come from the 2025 GTM phase and should be attributed as early-stage, founder-assisted pipeline rather than closed revenue.
